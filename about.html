About Culturei

We believe in the power of clean — without compromise. Our Mzansi product line is born from a love for the earth, crafted with plant-based ingredients and a commitment to excellence that goes beyond the bottle.

Proudly South African · Established 2024

Our Story

Culturei was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to provide premium, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that South Africans can trust. We saw a gap in the market for high-quality products that are both effective and kind to the environment.

Today, our Mzansi product line is trusted by households, guesthouses, laundries, and businesses across the country. Every product is carefully formulated using locally sourced ingredients and sustainable practices.
