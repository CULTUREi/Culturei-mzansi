About Culturei

Who We Are

Clean Homes. Simple Products. Real Mzansi Value.

Culturei is a South African cleaning brand focused on affordable, reliable, and practical household products. We help families keep their homes clean without overspending.

Our flagship brand, Mzansi, represents the spirit of South Africa - resilient, vibrant, and effective. Every product is formulated with care, using eco-friendly ingredients that are tough on dirt but gentle on your wallet.

Built for real households that need products that work, last longer, and stay affordable. From our humble beginnings in Cape Town, we've grown into a trusted name that families across Mzansi rely on daily.
