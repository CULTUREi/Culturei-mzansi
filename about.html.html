Who We Are

Culturei is a South African company focused on supplying high-quality cleaning products and detergent solutions for households, laundries, guesthouses, lodges, schools, and businesses.

Our mission is simple: provide affordable cleaning products that deliver excellent results while helping customers reduce cleaning costs.

Through our Mzansi detergent range, we help customers remove tough stains, maintain hygiene standards, and keep fabrics fresh and clean.

Our Vision

To become a trusted cleaning solutions brand serving homes and businesses throughout South Africa.
