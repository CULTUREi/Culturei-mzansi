  <h1>How to Remove Grease Stains from Work Uniforms Using Mzansi</h1>

  <p>
    Grease stains are tough because oil bonds with fabric fibers. Normal washing often fails.
  </p>

  <h3>Simple Method</h3>

  <p>
    1. Apply Mzansi directly on the stain<br>
    2. Rub gently<br>
    3. Wait 5–10 minutes<br>
    4. Wash normally<br>
    5. Do not dry until stain is gone
  </p>

  <h3>Why Mzansi Works</h3>
